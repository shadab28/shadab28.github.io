About

Markets first. Systems second. Risk always.

I started on a rates desk. From 2021 to 2024 at Futures First I traded SOFR and Fed Funds futures (SR3, SR1 and ZQ), built fair-value models for the illiquid back of the curve, and tuned spread execution on Trading Technologies’ Autospreader and ADL. The lesson that stuck: a model is worth what it can be filled at.

I then moved to systematic trading in Indian equity derivatives at NX Block Trades. There I owned the full loop for equity-futures and NIFTY / BANKNIFTY options strategies: research, walk-forward validation, cost modelling, and automated execution, with Kelly-based sizing and Monte Carlo drawdown estimates.

Today I trade foreign exchange at Aspora, on cross-border payment flows. The work is capital-flow forecasting across currency corridors, FX execution and liquidity analysis, and treasury analytics. Alongside the desk I research systematic macro and derivatives strategies, and I publish the method and its limitations next to the result.

The common thread is that the view comes from market structure, the test decides whether it survives, and the risk is set before the trade.

Pure Alpha

Systematic equity futures

Market
NSE single-stock futures, nearest expiry, on the point-in-time F&O list (304 stocks over 2016–2026)
Strategy
Long-only trend continuation on 15-minute bars
Question
Does intraday trend continuation in stock futures survive costs across a decade and a full universe?
Method
Frozen specification re-run on the stocks and futures lots that existed at the time: 16.6M bars, 25,123 positions. Built on a Python, Flask and PostgreSQL platform with paper and live broker adapters and a kill switch

03Design only

Synthetic Index Wheel

NIFTY / BANKNIFTY

Market
NIFTY and BANKNIFTY index options and futures
Strategy
Short OTM put. If it settles in the money in cash, buy the next-month future at the expiry close and write OTM calls against it
Question
How do you rebuild the wheel’s delivery leg when index options settle in cash?
Method
Replication design covering futures basis, carry, roll cost and lot-size granularity
Finding
Carry costs roughly 40–45 bp a month against holding stock, and one lot is about ₹20 lakh of notional, so sizing is lumpy

Framework only. No historical backtest has been run.

Approach

Research philosophy

Four rules that decide whether a result is worth trading.

01

Data before story

Rebuild the universe as it stood on each date, and trust a price only once it reconciles with the exchange file. A narrative earns a test. It does not replace one.

02

Risk before return

Size from the loss the book can absorb, then look at the return. Leverage is a parameter to test, not a setting to assume.

03

Execution matters

A backtest that fills at mid is a forecast of a different strategy. Costs, slippage, participation limits and settlement mechanics go in before the first result is read.

04

Validate everything

Checks run against independent data. Parameters chosen in-sample are disclosed as in-sample, and when a check fails, its effect is measured and reported.
